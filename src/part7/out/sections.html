Open Questions for Open-Ended Problems

To close, some open questions for open-ended problems.
Question #1
Does learning from really, really smart people in diverse areas help?
  • How to create smart-people data scalably?
  • How to measure the gap in thinking between LLMs and these really, really smart people?
First: does learning from people help?
More precisely: does learning from really, really smart people, in diverse areas, help?
If so, how do we create data from such people at scale?
And how do we measure the gap in thinking between LLMs and these people?
Question #2
How can agents better understand the vast open world?
  • How to enable agents to grasp more pieces of reality?
  • Given a huge dump of raw information, how to find subtle signals scattered all over it?
Second: how do agents get a better understanding of the vast open world?
How do we let agents grasp more pieces of reality?
And given a huge dump of raw information, how do they find the subtle signals scattered all over it?
Question #3
How should agents deal with humans?
  • Anything involving humans is open-ended!
  • Can agents orchestrate a society of humans?
Third: how do agents deal with humans?
Anything that involves humans is open-ended.
Can an agent orchestrate a whole society of humans?
